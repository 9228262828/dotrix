DOTRIX PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Summary: DOTRIX is designed as an offline-first reflex game. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, a remote backend, cloud sync, ads, or behavioral analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DOTRIX, package com.dotrix.reflex, handles information when you use the mobile game.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the current core version of DOTRIX and these informational pages. If future versions materially change data handling, this Policy should be updated before release.

3. Game Data

DOTRIX stores limited gameplay information locally, such as best scores, games played, total successful taps, best combo, and game settings.

4. Classic Mode Data

The app may store your best Classic Mode score locally on your device.

5. Time Rush Data

The app may store your best Time Rush score locally on your device.

6. Endless Mode Data

The app may store your best Endless Mode score locally on your device.

7. Games Played

The app may keep a local count of the number of games you have completed.

8. Successful Taps

The app may keep a local total of successful target taps for statistics.

9. Best Combo

The app may store your highest combo achieved across game sessions.

10. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode preference may be stored locally so the app can remember your selected appearance.

11. Haptic Feedback Preference

Your haptic-feedback preference may be stored locally so the app can remember whether vibration feedback is enabled.

12. Local Storage

The current core version stores the gameplay information described above locally using application storage such as SharedPreferences.

13. No Account Required

You do not need to create a DOTRIX account, username, profile, or password to play the current core version.

14. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, analytics, cloud databases, or remote configuration.

15. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your gameplay progress to a DOTRIX-operated remote backend.

16. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide DOTRIX-operated cloud synchronization of scores or preferences between devices.

17. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain DOTRIX-operated advertising.

18. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

19. No Cross-App Tracking

DOTRIX is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

20. No Sale of Personal Information

DOTRIX does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored gameplay information described in this Policy.

21. Permissions

The described core features do not require location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar, photos, media, or payment permissions.

22. Location

The current core version does not require precise or approximate location information.

23. Camera and Photos

The current core version does not require camera access or photo-library access.

24. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access, voice recording, or audio capture.

25. Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your device contacts.

26. Files and Media

The current core version does not require access to your personal files, documents, images, or media library for normal gameplay.

27. Payments

The current core version does not require payment-card details, banking credentials, or payment account information.

28. Haptic Feedback

If haptic feedback is enabled, DOTRIX may use the device's built-in vibration or haptic capability to provide touch feedback during gameplay.

29. Timers and Gameplay Logic

DOTRIX uses local timers and game-state logic to control target timing, game duration, lives, combos, and score progression. This processing occurs on the device.

30. Statistics

Statistics such as games played, total successful taps, best combo, and best scores are calculated from locally stored gameplay data.

31. Data Retention

Locally stored scores and preferences generally remain on your device until you reset progress, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

32. Reset Progress

DOTRIX includes a reset option that deletes locally stored scores and gameplay statistics while leaving app functionality available.

33. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling DOTRIX may remove locally stored gameplay information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

34. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by DOTRIX's core game logic.

35. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include DOTRIX-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover scores or preferences after deletion, device loss, app removal, or storage failure.

36. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode or biometric lock.

37. Children

DOTRIX is a casual reflex game intended for a general audience. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, profiles, forms, or messaging features in the current core version.

38. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process technical, account, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. Those practices are separate from DOTRIX's local gameplay data.

39. Google Play

If you download DOTRIX through Google Play, Google may process app-installation, account, device, purchase, security, or diagnostic information under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

40. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit DOTRIX gameplay data.

41. Support Communications

If you contact us at adampainting2005@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, secret keys, or other highly sensitive information.

42. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process. The current core app does not maintain a DOTRIX-operated cloud database of your gameplay progress.

43. International Use

You may use DOTRIX in different countries. Local gameplay data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

44. Future Features

If future releases add accounts, cloud sync, multiplayer, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, leaderboards, online services, or additional permissions, this Policy should be updated before release.

45. Your Choices

You can choose whether to enable haptic feedback, change appearance settings, reset progress, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or stop using DOTRIX.

46. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DOTRIX changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change.

47. Contact

For privacy questions about DOTRIX, contact adampainting2005@gmail.com.
